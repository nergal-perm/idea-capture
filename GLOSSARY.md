# Idea Capture

The language of capturing source material and original thoughts for later processing in an Inbox.

## Language

**Capture**:
A single saved submission containing one or more of source material, attachments, and an original comment. Its parts belong together and produce one fleeting note.
_Avoid_: Note (before delivery)

**Source material**:
Text, a link, and available source metadata received from another application. It remains distinguishable from the author's own comment.
_Avoid_: Comment

**Comment**:
The author's own thought accompanying source material or attachments, or submitted on its own. It may be typed or recorded as speech.
_Avoid_: Source material

**Attachment**:
An original file saved as part of a capture and linked from its resulting fleeting note. Preserving a file does not imply extracting or interpreting its content.
_Avoid_: Extracted content

**Fleeting note**:
The temporary Inbox item created from a capture for later processing in PersonalOS. One note may refer to multiple attachments; after processing, retained material belongs in permanent locations under the vault's storage rules.
_Avoid_: Capture (for the resulting Inbox item)

**Delivered**:
The state in which creation of the resulting fleeting note has been confirmed. Network availability or receipt by the server alone does not establish delivery.
_Avoid_: Sent, uploaded (as synonyms for delivered)
